About the Event

Where Healthcare Innovation Meets Kerala

WELLEXPO 2026 is South India's premier healthcare and pharmaceutical exhibition, curated by The Shows by Wildeye. Over three transformative days, 7,500+ healthcare professionals, hospital decision-makers, and global innovators converge at KINFRA International Exhibition Centre in Kochi.

Part of The Shows by Wildeye portfolio, WELLEXPO takes a balanced approach serving B2B and institutional markets — with a focus on resilience, recurring revenue, and cross-selling synergies. With a 72% exhibitor retention rate and delegates arriving from 15+ countries, WELLEXPO consistently delivers measurable ROI through immersive B2B experiences, clinical education, and commercial deal-making at the highest level.

Regional Advantage

Kerala Healthcare Market Deep Dive

Kerala stands as one of India's most advanced healthcare ecosystems — combining world-class medical infrastructure, a globally renowned skilled workforce, and strong government policy support.

  • India's leading glove manufacturer, with significant export partnerships across Europe, North America, and Southeast Asia
  • Proposed Pharma Park in Kakkanad, Kochi — strategically adjacent to KINFRA Exhibition Centre — set to become a major pharmaceutical manufacturing hub
  • Skilled medical workforce backed by strong academic infrastructure including premier nursing colleges, medical universities, and biotech institutes
  • Proactive state government support with dedicated industry policy and fast-track approvals for healthcare investments and medical device manufacturers
  • Export-oriented manufacturing with established global partnerships, positioning Kochi as a gateway for international healthcare trade in South Asia
🏥 Healthcare Infrastructure
Kerala has one of the highest doctor-to-patient ratios in India. The state is home to over 300 multi-speciality hospitals and is a leading destination for medical tourism, attracting over 50,000 international patients annually.
💊 Pharmaceutical Ecosystem
The proposed Pharma Park in Kakkanad, Kochi will create a dedicated zone for pharmaceutical manufacturing, R&D, and biotech companies — positioning Kerala as the pharmaceutical hub of South India.
🔬 Research & Innovation
Strong academic and research infrastructure with premier institutions including AIIMS Thiruvananthapuram, Amrita Institute of Medical Sciences, and Government Medical Colleges driving clinical research and healthcare innovation.
🌐 Strategic Location
Kochi's Cochin International Airport connects directly to the Gulf, Southeast Asia, Europe, and beyond. The city serves as the commercial capital of Kerala with excellent logistics infrastructure for international trade.

About the Organiser

Your Gateway to Global Markets

The Shows by Wildeye is an integrated exhibition and event management company focused on creating impactful trade shows, immersive exhibitions, and large-scale public events. The company specialises in organising industry-focused exhibitions that connect businesses, innovators, and audiences through thoughtfully designed event experiences.

Beyond event organisation, The Shows by Wildeye operates as an integrated event ticketing and audience management platform, enabling seamless ticket sales, attendee registration, and event access management for exhibitions, conferences, and entertainment events across India and internationally.

Registered with the Ministry of MSME (Govt. of India) and recognised under the DPIIT Startup India initiative, the company combines exhibition expertise, digital ticketing infrastructure, and intelligent automation tools to deliver end-to-end solutions for event organisers, venues, and brands.
